# Human Authorship, Research Control, and AI-Provider Non-Claim Notice

**Project:** VIBE MATH / RC-Discovery  
**Human author and research director:** Christopher Gardner  
**Effective notice date:** 31 October 2026  
**Status:** canonical repository-governance statement; not legal advice

## 1. Human control of the research programme

Christopher Gardner is the human researcher who selected the questions, directed the programme, accepted or rejected conjectures, required counterexamples, chose the canonical statements, interpreted the evidence, controlled publication decisions, and assumes responsibility for the repository.

Artificial-intelligence systems have been used as research tools for activities including:

- exploratory dialogue;
- derivation assistance;
- code drafting;
- numerical experiment design;
- adversarial proof checking;
- literature triage;
- technical editing;
- Lean formalisation support;
- repository maintenance.

The use of an AI tool does not make the tool or its provider a researcher, author, co-author, inventor, assignee, sponsor, institution, reviewer, endorser, or owner of this project.

## 2. Express non-claim position

No permission, assignment, licence, waiver, acknowledgement, or representation is granted by Christopher Gardner or RC-Discovery to OpenAI—or to any other AI model provider—merely because its services were used to assist the work.

In particular, OpenAI and other AI providers are not authorised by this project to assert or imply, solely on the basis of tool use, any claim of:

- authorship or co-authorship;
- inventorship or co-inventorship;
- ownership or co-ownership;
- assignment or beneficial title;
- research priority;
- exclusivity;
- institutional affiliation;
- sponsorship or commissioning;
- endorsement;
- control over publication;
- entitlement to royalties, licensing income, credit, or decision-making authority;
- status as the originator of VIBE MATH, RC-Discovery, or the combined theorem candidate.

No repository acknowledgement of AI assistance should be interpreted as transferring any of those positions.

## 4. Rights retained by service providers

Nothing in this notice claims ownership of OpenAI's or another provider's models, services, software, trademarks, infrastructure, documentation, or other provider property.

The project claims only its own human-directed research materials and whatever rights may lawfully subsist in them. The fact that AI output may be non-unique is expressly recognised. Questions of copyright subsistence, patent inventorship, protectability, ownership, and enforceability remain governed by applicable law and must not be inferred from this notice alone.

## 5. Disclosure is not attribution of ownership

The repository will continue to disclose material AI assistance honestly. That disclosure serves research transparency and reproducibility. It does not confer authorship, inventorship, ownership, priority, endorsement, or institutional standing on an AI system or provider.

Permitted description:

> Christopher Gardner directed and authored the research programme. AI systems, including OpenAI tools, were used as research instruments for derivation support, coding, numerical testing, adversarial auditing, literature triage, editing, and formalisation assistance.

Prohibited description without Christopher Gardner's express written authorisation:

> OpenAI authored, invented, owns, originated, sponsored, or controls RC-Discovery, VIBE MATH, or its theorem candidates.

## 6. Responsibility and verification

Christopher Gardner and any future human co-authors remain responsible for:

- deciding which statements are advanced;
- verifying mathematical correctness;
- correcting errors;
- making accurate prior-art disclosures;
- complying with publication and research-integrity standards;
- obtaining legal advice where ownership, licensing, patent, or contractual questions matter.

AI-generated or AI-assisted material is not treated as established merely because a model produced or endorsed it.

## 7. No legal overstatement

This notice is not:

- a legal opinion;
- a copyright registration;
- a patent filing;
- an inventorship determination;
- a contractual amendment;
- a guarantee of exclusive ownership;
- a restriction on non-waivable rights;
- a substitute for advice from a qualified solicitor, barrister, patent attorney, or intellectual-property specialist.

Its purpose is to make the project's attribution and non-claim position explicit: **OpenAI and other AI providers supplied tools; they are not designated as claimants to this research.**
