## Language policy

- **Known** — supported by existing literature.
- **Derived** — follows from known mathematics or an internally checked argument.
- **Provisionally novel** — precise result with an internal proof, but no independent novelty confirmation.
- **Open** — conjecture or research question.
- **Refuted** — false statement retained for audit trail.
