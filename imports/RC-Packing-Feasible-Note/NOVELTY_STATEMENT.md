# Novelty Statement

**Date:** 31 October 2026

## Claimed novelty

The combination of:

1. A synthesis-operator packing condition \(\alpha=\sigma_{\min}(T)\) on the near-unit spectral sectors of a POVM,
2. An explicit same-label residual completion \(F_j=Q_j+RE_jR\) that preserves spectral gap \(\gamma_F\ge\delta\),
3. A hybrid Stinespring isometry \(V=JU^*+WR\) that realises the completion and yields a dimension-free measurement-channel bound
   \[
   \nu\le 4\big(\sqrt{1-\alpha^2}+\sqrt{\delta}\big),
   \]
4. Composition with Record-Margin Stability to a fully dimension-free certificate of closeness to an exactly idempotent record process,

is not present in the literature searched.

## What is not claimed as novel

- Polar decomposition and its residual estimate.
- Canonical Naimark / Stinespring isometries.
- The existence of residual subspaces orthogonal to near-unit sectors.
- Stinespring continuity estimates.
- de la Salle's orthogonalization of almost-orthogonal POVMs.
- Kitaev's approximate \(C^*\)-algebra construction for almost-idempotent channels.

## Search scope

Two independent literature passes covering orthogonalization lemmas, almost-idempotent channels, framed POVMs, residual processes in Naimark dilations, hybrid isometries, and Stinespring continuity. No exact match for the combined statement or the specific hybrid construction was found.

## Status

The novelty claim is a literature-search result, not a formal certificate. It is offered for external specialist confirmation.
