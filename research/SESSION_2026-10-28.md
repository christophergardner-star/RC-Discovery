# Research Log — 28 October 2026

Compact reconstruction of the productive branch of the session.

## 1. Initial unification attempt

RC was explored as a language in which sequential alternatives compose multiplicatively while unresolved alternatives can combine additively.

That line reproduced familiar distinctions between coherent alternatives, environmental records, objective/broadcast information and thermodynamic irreversibility.

No novelty claim survived this stage.

## 2. Important correction: three regimes, not two

The session separated:

1. coherence,
2. objectivity / redundant records,
3. irreversibility / loss of recoverability.

This prevented the incorrect identification of decoherence, classicality and thermodynamic irreversibility as one transition.

## 3. Redundancy and erasure

A proposed “redundancy gap”

\[
\sum_i H(M_i)-H(M_1,\ldots,M_R)
\]

was recognised as existing information-theoretic structure rather than a new RC quantity.

## 4. Finite relation search

Toy finite models tested whether symmetry forced reciprocity.

Several early formulations were too weak or too strong. The surviving finite statement — transitivity plus local in/out balance forcing reciprocal components — was recognised as elementary and not promoted.

## 5. Almost-idempotent channel branch

The target shifted to exact rounding:

> When does approximate compositional closure imply proximity to an exact projection?

Known work supplies two boundaries:

- the general quantum problem has a negative answer;
- recent metastability work restores exact rounding under bounded slow dimension.

This exposed a classification question.

## 6. Record geometry

For

\[
\Phi(\rho)=\sum_j\operatorname{Tr}(P_j\rho)\sigma_j,
\]

the session introduced

\[
\kappa
=
\min_j\frac12
\inf_{\omega\in\operatorname{conv}\{\sigma_i:i\neq j\}}
\|\sigma_j-\omega\|_1
\]

and

\[
\eta=\|\Phi^2-\Phi\|_\diamond.
\]

This yielded the internally proved bound

\[
\|\Phi-\Pi\|_\diamond
\le
\sqrt{\frac{2\eta}{\kappa}}
\]

for an exact idempotent \(\Pi\).

## 7. Sharpness

A two-record qubit family attains equality exactly.

## 8. Numerical reproduction

A fresh implementation with seed 20261028 tested 10,000 full-rank two-record models across dimensions

\[
2,3,5,10,20.
\]

Observed in the local rerun used to prepare this repository:

- violations: 0,
- maximum actual-distance / theorem-bound ratio: approximately 0.999925603293.

The analytic qubit family reaches ratio exactly 1.

## 9. Current interpretation

The strongest defensible statement is:

> Within a sharp separated-record subclass, objective record geometry converts approximate repeatability into a dimension-independent guarantee of proximity to exact repeatable dynamics.

This does not establish RC as a fundamental theory and does not solve the quantum measurement problem.

## 10. Current target

Map the boundary between negative general exact-rounding results and positive record-margin exact rounding.

The next especially interesting extension is to derive how environmental redundancy changes effective record margin.
