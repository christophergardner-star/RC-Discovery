## 8. What this does and does not solve

### New positive result

The outcome-count bottleneck for the measurement-channel move is removed **for an explicit Naimark-completion rounding**.

This is enough to establish a dimension-free nearby repeatability-admissible measurement channel in the well-packed regime.

### Still to audit

1. The proof requires an independent reconstruction / literature veto.
2. This is not yet proved for the specific residual formula
   [
   F_j=Q_j+RE_jR.
   ]
   The theorem constructs a different residual completion through the Naimark dilation.
3. To feed the existing RC spectral-gap Record-Margin theorem with only ((alpha,delta)), one still wants a quantitative lower bound on the spectral gap of the newly constructed (F_j), or one keeps that gap as an additional parameter.
4. Full Lean formalisation remains open.
